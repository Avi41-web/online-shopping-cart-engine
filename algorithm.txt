1. Algorithm
1. Start the program.
2. Store the available products, names, and prices.
3. Create an empty shopping cart.
4. Display the menu with five options:
   - View products
   - Add a product to the cart
   - View the cart
   - Remove a product
   - Checkout and exit
5. Ask the customer to enter a menu choice.
6. If the choice is 1, display all available products and prices.
7. If the choice is 2, ask for a product number and quantity, validate the input, and add the product to the cart.
8. If the choice is 3, display the cart items, quantities, and subtotal.
9. If the choice is 4, ask for a product number and remove that product from the cart if it exists.
10. If the choice is 5, calculate the subtotal, apply a 10% discount if the subtotal is ₹2000 or more, display the final bill, and exit.
11. If the customer enters an invalid menu choice, display an error message.
12. Repeat the menu until the customer selects checkout and exit.
13. Stop the program.
